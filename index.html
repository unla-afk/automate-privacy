Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

The short version

AutoMate keeps your information on your own phone. We don't run accounts, we don't track you, and we don't sell or share your data. The details below explain exactly what happens to any information you put into the app.

What we store, and where

Everything you add — your vehicles, their details (including VIN if you enter one), photos, nicknames, odometer readings and service history — is stored locally on your device. It is not uploaded to us or to any server, and we cannot see it. If you delete the app, that data is removed with it.

We don't have accounts or profiles

You don't sign in, and we don't create a profile about you. Because your data lives only on your device, we hold no personal information about you on our side.

Analytics and tracking

We do not use analytics, advertising trackers, or behaviour-tracking tools. We do not monitor how you use the app.

When information leaves your device

There are only a few times anything leaves your phone, and only when you choose to do something:

• VIN lookup: if you enter a VIN, it is sent to the free public United States government vehicle database (NHTSA) to identify the vehicle. No account or personal details are sent — only the VIN.

• Vehicle issue data: the app downloads an updated list of common vehicle issues so the information stays current. This is a one-way download — none of your data is sent as part of it.

• Feedback: if you tap "Send feedback", your email app opens a message to us. We only receive what you choose to write and send. We include your app version and device type in the draft to help us fix problems — you can delete that before sending.

Camera and photos

If you add a photo of your vehicle, the app asks permission to use your camera or photo library. Photos you add are stored on your device only and are never uploaded.

Reminders

If you turn on service reminders, they are scheduled and shown by your device. We don't send these from a server and they involve no personal data leaving your phone.

Children

AutoMate is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we'll update this policy and the date at the top. Significant changes will be noted in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at josh@power-consultation.com and we'll help.
